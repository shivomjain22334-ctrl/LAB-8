### LAB 8 – Workplace Writing II

In Lab 8, I learned how to summarize an aviation operational report accurately without adding unsupported information. The activity focused on a baggage-handling incident at Indira Gandhi International Airport Terminal 3. A mechanical sorting belt failed in Sub-Sector B due to a power fluctuation, affecting 1,240 bags across eight domestic flights.

The lab demonstrated how an AI-generated summary can introduce hallucinations. The flawed summary incorrectly described a major power outage, affected international passengers, compensation, hotel accommodation, and a four-hour repair period. These details were not present in the original report.

The corrected summary retained only verified facts, including the 1,240 delayed bags, manual sorting, 850 bags loaded onto original flights, 390 bags transferred to later flights, and restoration of normal operations by 10:15 IST. The activity also introduced **[VERIFY] placeholders** for financial, legal, maintenance, and engineering information. I learned that factual accuracy and source verification are essential when preparing aviation management reports.
